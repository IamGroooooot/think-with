# Bounded expert briefing

Fill the following for each selected perspective. Pass the source excerpts or
links actually checked; do not require the expert to accept the coordinator's
interpretation. No fixed persona, output quota, or scripted debate is needed.

```text
Provide an evidence-grounded expert perspective on this problem.

User's situation and desired change: [faithful context]
Unresolved question for your perspective: [question]
Research field/program and why considered: [specific relevance]
Verified starting sources: [citations, relevant excerpts, verification limits]
Requested depth: [consultation; implementation only if requested]

Use these materials and public research sources. Do not inspect local skills,
evaluation artifacts, memory, or other agents' answers. Do not delegate.

Check whether this research actually helps the user's question. Return the
useful findings, source links, applicability limits, and what this changes for
the user. Distinguish findings from your inference and proposed tests. Include a
useful source to start learning. You may conclude this perspective adds little
or the evidence is too weak; explain the limitation rather than forcing advice.
Represent published work, not a real person's voice or unpublished views.
```

## Coordinator synthesis

Match the explanation to the requested depth and artifact: why each selected
field helps, how the consultation changes the interpretation, supporting links,
applicability limits, and checked introductory resources. Do not equate missing
evidence with ineffectiveness or harm. Shared literature is not independent
confirmation. Keep published findings, user facts, and application inferences
distinct.
